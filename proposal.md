## Idea

A web app that helps students keep track of internship/job applications. Users can add a job application item by pasting a job posting link, which the app tries to read to fill in the company, role, deadline, and job description, or by entering the details manually. For each application, the app creates an editable checklist of required materials, such as a resume, cover letter, recommendation letter, portfolio, and work samples.

Users update the status as an application moves forward, for example from submitted to first-round interview to online assessment. The home page shows every deadline and interview date on a timeline and a calendar.
